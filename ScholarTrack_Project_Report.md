## ABSTRACT
ScholarTrack – Smart Scholarship Eligibility, Verification & Tracking Portal is an end-to-end full-stack web application engineered using React.js, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB to digitize, streamline, and audit the scholarship application lifecycle. The primary novelty of ScholarTrack is the automatic detection of mismatches between student-entered information and uploaded scholarship documents before final verification, accompanied by clear explanations and a guided in-place correction process.
